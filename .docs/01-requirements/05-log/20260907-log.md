## Evidence base fixed for the spec

The spec is written from the **YummyNaKha! survey of Sep 6–7 2026** — six
respondents, anonymised U1–U6. No interview data was invented and no email or
identifying information was recorded.

Three free-text answers are quoted verbatim: U1's "Accuracy.", U2's "Is it
really trustworthy and accurate?", and U4's hesitation statement about
inaccurate information, too few reviews, and difficulty of use. Every other
answer is paraphrased and labelled as a survey response, never as a quotation.

Demand across the six responses: translation 5/6 · menu prices 4/6 ·
menu-photo upload 3/6 · preference- or allergy-related help 3/6.

Pains derived:

- **P1** difficulty understanding unfamiliar Thai menu information without help
- **P2** dependence on restaurant staff, friends, Google Translate, Google
  Search, and dish pictures to reach a decision
- **P3** wanting help deciding whether an unfamiliar dish matches personal
  preferences or restrictions
- **P4** accuracy and trust deciding willingness to rely on AI menu information
- **P5** wanting a direct photo-based way to process an unfamiliar menu

## Decisions taken while writing the spec

1. **F1 (account and sign-in) is Must** — it sits beside the menu chain rather
   than inside it, but F2's "saved once" profile and LR9's consent records both
   need a persistent identity. Authentication mechanism left `TBD`; it is not
   fixed by the Brief or the Charter.
2. **Three Musts exist only to serve P4** — F10 (uncertain information resolves
   to 🟡, never 🟢), F11 (confirm-with-staff wording), F12 (show what a result
   was based on). P4 is the strongest evidence signal, 3 of 6 free-text answers,
   but had no feature in either source document. This is the one place the spec
   adds product beyond the Brief and Charter.
3. **NFR8 (ease of use) added as `TBD`** — U4 named "difficult to use" as a
   reason to hesitate, so leaving usability unmeasured was not acceptable; no
   number was invented.
4. **Provider-neutral wording throughout** — no OCR/AI provider is named
   anywhere, because no committed project document selects one.
5. **Menu images discarded after processing by default** (LR5). Permanent
   menu-image storage is not an MVP requirement.
6. **Scan history stays Could** (F20, B32), outside core scope, as the Brief
   defines it.
7. **U4's "not enough reviews"** is recorded in P4's evidence but has no
   functional requirement behind it — social proof is outside the core
   workflow. F21 (rating a recommendation) is the nearest item and is a Could.
