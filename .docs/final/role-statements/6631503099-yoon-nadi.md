**Role:** Designer · Team 5-Idiots · YummyNaKha!

## What I owned

I owned the user-facing design of YummyNaKha!: how a diner moves from a food
profile to a Thai menu photo, to three result groups, to ordering in Thai.

- **Prototype:** [Figma Make — "Execute action", Version 7](https://www.figma.com/make/NEV4YbRQ9WN1m3MDkAdZsp/Execute-action)
- **Design document:** [03-prototype.md](../../02-design/03-prototype.md) — 12
  screens (S1–S12) with copy, states and traces to F/LR ids, plus the
  prototype-label ↔ requirement-term table (Top Picks / Check First / Avoid).
- **Screens:** the 18 exported screenshots in
  [02-design/images/](../../02-design/images/).
- **Gap list G1–G8:** I recorded where the prototype did not yet meet the spec
  (e.g. no consent gate, no account deletion) instead of hiding it.
- I aligned the feature list and user journey to prototype terminology
  (commit `0dfdc2f`).
- Final docs: [user guide](../user-guide.md),
  [case study](../case-study.md),
  [secondary evidence review](../../01-requirements/02-secondary-review.md).

My main design rule was safety wording: no screen says a dish is "safe", no
score is shown, and warnings point to restaurant staff.
